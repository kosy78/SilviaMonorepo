## Design Context

### Users

DespreSine is used by adults, young adults, and adolescents aged 16+ who may
be considering psychoanalytic psychotherapy or counseling. Visitors may arrive
without a clear way to describe what they are experiencing. The interface
should help them understand the practice, reflect without being categorized,
and find a low-pressure path to contact.

### Brand Personality

Calm, discreet, and editorial. The voice is emotionally intelligent and
professional without becoming institutional, approachable without becoming
casual, and informative without becoming persuasive.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, typography-led editorial composition with generous whitespace,
precise rules, and restrained interaction. Black, warm white, and tinted
neutrals remain dominant. Color is reserved for meaningful categorization in
the guided reflection: muted moss for the body, muted plum for emotional
states, warm ochre for relationships, and a neutral treatment for uncertainty.
Avoid wellness imagery, bright palettes, decorative gradients, clinical
dashboard conventions, and generic medical or SaaS styling.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve trust before pursuing novelty; every visual choice should feel
   calm, legible, and deliberate.
2. Keep the guided reflection explicitly descriptive and non-diagnostic; never
   make frequency resemble severity, pathology, or a patient category.
3. Use color only when it improves meaning or wayfinding, and always pair it
   with text, counts, or structure.
4. Let typography, spacing, and editorial rhythm carry the brand; avoid
   ornamental cards, effects, and unnecessary motion.
5. Design mobile-first with accessible contrast, visible focus, generous touch
   targets, keyboard support, and reduced-motion compatibility.

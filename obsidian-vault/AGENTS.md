# Obsidian Vault

## Scope

This directory is the project's thinking and planning layer. It is intended to
be opened as an Obsidian vault and may later be connected to agent tooling.

Use it for:

- strategy and roadmap notes;
- SEO and topic research;
- website copy drafts;
- blog ideas and editorial planning;
- Instagram and campaign planning;
- guided reflection content design;
- competitor and reference research;
- meeting notes;
- decision records;
- reusable prompts and workflow documentation.

The vault contains working knowledge, not production code or approved binary
assets. Final executable campaign artifacts belong in `campaigns/`; runnable
website work belongs in `website/`; approved visual files belong in
`brand-assets/`.

## Note conventions

- Write notes in Markdown.
- Use a clear title and one primary purpose per note.
- Prefer descriptive filenames over generic names such as `ideas-2.md`.
- Use ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) where dates are part of a filename or property.
- Use Obsidian links to connect related decisions, campaigns, pages, and
  research.
- Mark the state of actionable material: `draft`, `in-review`, `approved`,
  `published`, or `archived`.
- Include source URLs and access dates for external research.
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and decisions.
- When a decision supersedes an earlier one, link the records instead of
  silently rewriting history.

Do not create a complicated taxonomy before enough content exists to justify
it. Prefer a small, discoverable structure with indexes and links.

## Canonical content

Draft and refine strategy here. Once content becomes implementation-owned,
record where the canonical version lives:

- website copy or code: `website/`;
- executable campaign material: `campaigns/`;
- approved visual asset: `brand-assets/`;
- operational procedure: `operations/`.

The vault may link to those files but should not retain an unmarked duplicate
that can drift.

## Content and research rules

Follow all project facts and voice requirements from `/AGENTS.md`.

- Public Romanian copy must use correct diacritics.
- Do not invent biographical details, credentials, services, or outcomes.
- Date legal, SEO, platform, market, and competitor research.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources for professional, legal, privacy, and
  platform-policy claims.
- Clearly identify material that still requires Silvia's review.
- Keep diagnostic language out of public content drafts.

## Privacy

This vault must not become a client record system.

Never store:

- patient or client identities;
- session notes;
- case histories;
- contact-form submissions;
- email correspondence containing private information;
- guided-reflection answers;
- health information connected to a person;
- credentials, tokens, or account recovery information.

Use fictional or clearly generic examples when examples are necessary.

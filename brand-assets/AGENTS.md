# Brand Assets

## Scope

This directory owns visual identity source files and approved exports used by
the website and campaigns. It may eventually contain:

- logo and wordmark source files;
- typography references and licenses;
- approved photography;
- social templates;
- icons and illustrations;
- campaign exports;
- brand usage guidance.

Working strategy and commentary belong in `obsidian-vault/`. Website-specific
optimized copies may live in `website/`, but this directory should retain the
traceable approved source.

## V1 direction

- Black and white.
- Minimal and typography-led.
- Calm, professional, discreet, and elegant.
- Generous whitespace and restrained composition.
- One or two professional photographs at most for the initial website.
- Use `Silvia Dinu` as a wordmark until a logo is approved.
- Avoid wellness clichés, generic medical imagery, stock therapy scenes, bright
  palettes, and decorative complexity.

## Asset rules

- Do not label an asset `final` without an approval record.
- Preserve editable source files when licensing allows it.
- Use predictable filenames that identify subject, variation, size, and version.
- Keep web exports separate from editable masters once the asset library grows.
- Record creator, source, license, consent, usage restrictions, and approval
  state for third-party or commissioned assets.
- Do not commit unlicensed fonts, imagery, icons, or templates.
- Remove unnecessary embedded metadata from public exports when appropriate.
- Never place private client imagery or identifying information here.

## Handoff quality

Before handing an asset to the website or a campaign, verify:

- correct version and approval state;
- intended channel and dimensions;
- readable contrast;
- appropriate crop and safe area;
- file format and compression;
- meaningful alt-text guidance where relevant;
- license and consent for the intended use.

# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are Romanian-speaking adults, young adults, and adolescents
aged 16 and over who are considering psychotherapy for themselves. They may
arrive with a clearly named difficulty or only a sense that something needs
attention, and need enough clarity and trust to decide whether to initiate a
first conversation.

For adolescents aged 16–17, consent from the relevant parent or legal guardian
is required. Any public explanation of that requirement remains subject to
professional and legal review before publication.

## Product Purpose

DespreSine is Silvia Dinu's Romanian digital practice presence. It helps
visitors understand the available psychoanalytic psychotherapy and counseling,
Silvia's professional context, in-person and online formats, and how a first
contact can begin.

Success means helping appropriate visitors feel informed and safe enough to
initiate a low-pressure first contact. The product does not optimize for lead
volume, urgency, or pressure to book.

## Positioning

DespreSine is a calm, editorial, privacy-first independent practice presence,
not a clinic, marketplace, generic medical template, or wellness-influencer
brand. Its distinctive mechanism combines clear psychoanalytic context with an
anonymous guided reflection that supports orientation without diagnosing,
categorizing, or requiring contact details.

## Operating Context

- Visitors can learn about the practice, psychoanalytic psychotherapy and
  counseling, professional competencies, practical formats, and first contact.
- Sessions are available in person in București, zona Piața Victoriei / Banu
  Manta, and online, with equal visibility. The exact office address is not
  public in V1.
- The primary contact path is email-first, supplemented by a minimal contact
  form for visitors without a configured email client.
- Public content and campaigns are Romanian-only in V1 and use
  `dumneavoastră` by default.
- The website is pre-launch and password-protected until the public, legal,
  professional, privacy, and operational review gates are complete.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public service wording is `Psihoterapie psihanalitică și consiliere`.
- The public credential wording is `Psiholog cu drept de liberă practică` and
  `Psihoterapeut psihanalitic sub supervizare`; credentials, professional
  titles, legal statements, and regulatory claims require Silvia's approval
  before publication.
- The guided reflection is anonymous by default, runs locally where practical,
  does not store answers, does not require an email address, and must remain
  explicitly non-diagnostic.
- Reflection results may describe a local distribution across body, emotional
  states, and relationships, but must never imply severity, pathology,
  likelihood, or a patient type.
- Contact collects only the minimum needed for a response. It must not solicit
  diagnoses, medical history, detailed symptoms, emergency information,
  marketing consent, a phone number, or other unnecessary sensitive data.
- No public pricing, phone number, testimonials, review widgets, treatment
  promises, superiority claims, fear, urgency, shame, or emotional pressure in
  V1.
- Google Analytics is the only approved V1 analytics path and loads only after
  explicit consent. Guided-reflection answers, contact content, and personal or
  clinical data are excluded from analytics.
- The canonical public domain is `https://despresine.ro`; the Cloudflare Pages
  hostname is an operational fallback, not the public canonical identity.

## Brand Commitments

- `DespreSine` is the public wordmark and site name. `Silvia Dinu` remains
  visible as the practitioner subtitle, professional signature, and human
  source of trust.
- The voice is calm, emotionally intelligent, clear, restrained, professional,
  approachable, and informative rather than persuasive.
- Public copy uses correct Romanian diacritics and avoids diagnostic language,
  generic wellness slogans, sensationalism, and unsupported medical, legal, or
  psychological claims.
- The experience should feel serious, discreet, elegant, and human while
  keeping Silvia visibly connected to the DespreSine identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Repository-owned Romanian website copy, service information, professional
  context, legal-support pages, operational documentation, and dated decision
  records.
- An implemented guided-reflection flow, minimal contact form, blog foundation,
  consent layer, and responsive Astro website.
- Approved photography and brand assets stored in the repository where their
  use is documented.
- No testimonials, client stories, treatment-outcome evidence, comparative
  claims, public pricing, or marketplace-style social proof. Future work must
  not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Build trust through clarity, restraint, and factual orientation.
2. Make contact optional, gentle, and proportionate to visitor readiness.
3. Treat mental-health information as sensitive and collect the minimum data.
4. Support reflection without diagnosis, categorization, or implied clinical
   scoring.
5. Keep public claims grounded, reviewable, and visibly connected to Silvia's
   actual practice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support keyboard navigation, assistive technology, meaningful
document structure, sufficient contrast, visible focus, responsive layouts,
reduced-motion preferences, and reduced-transparency preferences where used.
The language must not assume a diagnosis, gender, technical knowledge, or a
visitor's readiness to begin therapy.

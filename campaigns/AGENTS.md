# Campaigns

## Scope

This directory contains campaign material that is ready to be reviewed,
executed, measured, or archived. It may eventually include Instagram campaigns,
Meta Ads, Google Ads, campaign landing-page copy, creative variants, experiment
plans, media notes, and performance reports.

Early exploration and working notes belong in `obsidian-vault/`. Move or link
material here when it becomes an actionable campaign artifact. Do not maintain
two competing canonical versions.

Read `/AGENTS.md` before working here. The root voice, privacy, credential, and
ethical-marketing constraints are mandatory.

## Required campaign context

Every campaign should state:

- campaign name and status;
- objective;
- intended audience;
- channel and placement;
- message and offer;
- destination or contact path;
- start and end dates;
- owner and reviewer;
- budget assumptions, if applicable;
- measurement plan;
- source material and approval state.

Use explicit statuses such as `draft`, `in-review`, `approved`, `live`,
`paused`, `completed`, and `archived`.

## Messaging rules

- Use Romanian with correct diacritics for public copy.
- Keep the tone calm, professional, restrained, and informative.
- Prefer clarity and relevance over hooks designed to provoke distress.
- Do not diagnose the audience or assert knowledge of an individual's mental
  state.
- Do not promise improvement, recovery, transformation, or guaranteed results.
- Do not use fear, shame, urgency, social pressure, or sensational symptom
  framing.
- Do not imply professional superiority.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, reviews, scarcity, credentials, or outcomes.
- Keep the primary conversion path email-first unless a later decision changes
  it.

## Sensitive targeting and platform rules

Mental-health interests, symptoms, and inferred conditions are sensitive. Do
not create audience definitions, personalization, tracking, or copy that
identifies or implies a person's health status.

Advertising platforms and applicable rules change. Verify current platform
policies and professional requirements at execution time, record the source and
date, and flag anything requiring Silvia's approval or legal review.

## Experiments and measurement

Define one primary question for each experiment. Change only enough variables to
make the result interpretable. Record:

- hypothesis;
- variants;
- audience and exclusions;
- success metric;
- guardrail metrics;
- planned duration or stopping rule;
- result;
- decision and follow-up.

Do not optimize only for clicks. Consider contact quality, trust, relevance,
privacy impact, and whether the message accurately represents the practice.

Do not store identifiable leads, private correspondence, form submissions, or
sensitive audience exports in this repository. Reports should use aggregated
data whenever possible.

## Asset handoff

Use approved visual sources from `brand-assets/`. Record dimensions, platform,
copy variant, export date, and approval status. Campaign-specific landing pages
implemented in the website must follow `website/AGENTS.md`.

# Weather

Weather describes the conditions in the atmosphere at a particular place and
time. It can change throughout the day as temperature, air pressure, wind, and
moisture shift.

Common weather conditions include:

- Sunny skies
- Cloud cover
- Rain or thunderstorms
- Snow or sleet
- Wind
- Fog

When checking a forecast, it is useful to look at the temperature, the chance
of precipitation, wind speed, humidity, and any weather warnings.

## New York Weather

New York experiences four distinct seasons. Summers are usually warm and humid,
while winters can be cold and snowy. Spring and autumn often bring milder
temperatures, though rain and sudden changes in conditions are common.

# Homepage closing invitation

The user approved a dedicated contact invitation after the homepage blog and
before the footer. It uses centered typography, the existing warm ivory and
olive palette, generous responsive spacing, and one action to `/contact/`,
matching the homepage hero. Both in-person and online sessions remain visible.

The homepage footer omits its duplicate meeting button. Other pages retain
the existing footer email action. Footer navigation, Instagram, legal links,
and leaf-shadow artwork remain in place.

Canonical copy and layout live in
`website/src/components/sections/HomepageClosing.astro`. This decision approves
local implementation; it does not record a production release.
